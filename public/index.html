How Hokm works
  • Four players in two teams; your partner sits across from you.
  • Cards are drawn until someone turns up an Ace — that player is the Hakem (the ruler).
  • The Hakem sees five cards and names the Hokm (trump) suit. Trump beats any other suit.
  • Everyone gets 13 cards. You must follow the suit that was led if you can. If you can't, play anything — trumping is optional.
  • Highest trump takes the trick; with no trump, the highest card of the led suit takes it. The winner leads next.
  • First team to 7 tricks wins the round: 1 point normally, 2 for a Kot (7–0 by the Hakem's team), 3 for a Hakem Koti (7–0 against the Hakem).
  • The Hakem keeps the role if their team wins; otherwise it passes to the left. First team to 7 points wins.
  • Two to eight players. Everyone starts off the board and races to square 100.
  • Roll the die and move that many squares.
  • Land on the foot of a ladder and you climb to its top.
  • Land on a snake's head and it bites — you slide all the way down to its tail.
  • You must land on 100 exactly. Overshoot and you bounce back off the end.
  • A six earns you another roll — but roll three sixes in a row and the whole turn is forfeit.
  • The board is drawn fresh every game, so nobody can learn it.
